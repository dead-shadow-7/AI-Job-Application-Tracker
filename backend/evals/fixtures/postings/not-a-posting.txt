Our Engineering Culture

At Brightmoor we believe that good software comes from small teams with real
ownership. Over the last four years we have grown from six engineers to forty,
and we have worked hard to keep the things that made the early days work:
short feedback loops, written decisions, and a bias toward shipping.

We write design documents for anything that will outlive a sprint. We review
each other's code carefully and kindly. We keep our on-call load low enough that
nobody dreads the pager.

If any of that sounds like the way you want to work, keep an eye on this space.
