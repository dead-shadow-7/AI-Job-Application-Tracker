About the job

partner with product and engineering to design experiments, build models, and
turn messy behavioural data into decisions the business can act on.

What you will do

- Build forecasting models in Python
- Present findings to senior stakeholders

What we are looking for
- 5+ years in an applied data science role
- Strong statistics and experimental design
- Fluency in Python and SQL

Join our team and help shape how we use data.
